Aventurine

also known as Kakavasha

About Aventurine

A senior manager in the IPC Strategic Investment Department and one of the Ten Stonehearts. He possesses an air of frivolity and doesn't shy away from taking risks. His constant smile makes it difficult for people to discern his true feelings.

Aventurine's characteristics

  1. His Cornerstone is the "Aventurine of Stratagems."
  2. He is a five star Imaginary character who follows the path of Preservation.
  3. He has short blonde hair and mesmerizing eyes that has the shades of purple and turquoise.
  4. He is an Avgin. Avgins were once one of the fragmented Sigonian clans located in Sigonia-IV. They were comprised of members of the Avgin race and worshipped Gaiathra Triclops.
  5. He won his position in the Ten Stonehearts by wagering against fate itself. He views life as a high-stakes, high-return investment, and he plays this particular gamble with masterful ease.
